*Настройка за показване на дата 25.05.2023:

    In the template document, while the merge field is selected, 
    do the following to display the field code:

        Press Shift + F9 on Windows.
        Press Fn + Shift + F9 on Mac.
    Remove "\*MERGEFORMAT" from the field code.
    Enter the switch in the field code. For example:
        Original field code: {MERGEFIELD myObject.myField  \*MERGEFORMAT} 
        Updated field code: {MERGEFIELD myObject.myField \# ###,###,###,#00.0}
    Update the field:
        Press F9 on Windows.
        Press Fn + F9 on Mac.
